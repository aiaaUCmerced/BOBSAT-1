# BOBSAT-1

BOBSAT-1 is a multiyear CubeSat project under the AIAA chapter of the University of California, Merced. This mission is designed to identify potentially harmful algal blooms across California's inland water bodies from space using low-cost COTS parts to pave the way for widespread potentially harmful algal bloom monitoring and advisory​. 
With the hope of establishing a CubeSat program for UC Merced, the goal BOBSAT is to provide students with opportunities to develop technical, professional, and specialized skills that can support their career development and prepare them for careers in industry.
